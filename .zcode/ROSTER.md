# Ω Team roster — the registry members are picked from

> Status: ACTIVE · created 2026-09-30, owner amendment: membership is **flexible per task** —
> a panel is picked for each session/task from this registry, with a **hard cap of 3
> deliberating agents**. No fixed cast is seated by default.
> Lineage: the four founding members carry the officer powers/limits of
> [board/CHARTER.md](board/CHARTER.md). New members join via the charter's expansion path
> (gap-ledger row → owner ratification → this registry + charter amended with lineage).

## Panel rules

1. **Cap: 3 deliberating agents per session or corridor — never more.** A bigger question is
   handled in more rounds, not a bigger cast.
2. **Composition follows the task.** One member = solo assessment (self-challenge must be
   recorded honestly). Two = proposer + challenger. Three = proposer + two challengers.
   Every member also establishes its own ground truth from the repository — there is no
   separate evidence-gatherer cast.
3. **Selection is recorded.** Whoever picks (a selector pass or the convening Steward) records
   *why* each member was picked; the minutes carry the panel composition and rationale.
4. **Read-only investigation may fan out** (readers, checkers in audit/research corridors) —
   the cap binds *deliberation*, not investigation. Deliberation is proposing, challenging,
   settling, deciding.
5. **The Steward chairs** and is never counted among the three; the chair organizes, records,
   commits, and holds no strategic vote.

## Expansion and retirement

Add a member only on evidence of need: a gap-ledger row, an owner ratification, then a row
here with specialty and pick-when criteria. Retire the same way, preserving lineage. No
silent self-mutation (GATE-07 discipline).

### Standing note — the swarm grows by capability, not by seat (2026-10-03)

The 2026-10-03 state assessment raised **nine** gap-ledger rows (G-00…G-08) and added **zero** members.
Both expansion proposals it raised were mechanised instead of staffed: G-01 became the
`Live writer corridors` table in [TRACKING.md](TRACKING.md#live-writer-corridors) and G-05
became the `omega-redproof` workflow. Seating an officer for either would have put an
execution role into a deliberation registry — this roster exists to seat panels that propose,
challenge and settle, and neither task is deliberation.

Two workflows were added in its place (`omega-redproof`, `omega-fixture`); the capability
assessment that produced them is in [TEAM.md](TEAM.md#the-swarm--which-agents-this-work-actually-needs-assessed-2026-10-03).
**A gap-ledger row is therefore not by itself a case for a new member** — the first question it
must survive is "is this deliberation at all?" and the second is "does a workflow close it?".

either is extended. Recorded here rather than decided, because retiring an instrument on a
single assessment's impression is exactly the kind of call that wants a challenge pass.
