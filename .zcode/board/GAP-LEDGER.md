## An honesty note this ledger owes itself

The 2026-10-03 adoption of the `forge-mine` corridor (D-TEAM-020) rests on a mutation test — two
mutations injected into the implementation, both observed to turn the suite red. **No receipt of
those runs was committed.** The work was done and the failures were seen, but "seen in a terminal
that was not saved" is exactly the category this project has been burned by, and an adversarial
audit (`CC61` subagent) correctly marked the claim UNSUPPORTED for want of evidence rather than
disproving it.

Two things follow, and both are about the instrument rather than the claim:

1. **`omega-redproof` exists precisely to produce this artifact** and did not get used here — the
   mutation test was done by hand, and by hand produced no receipt. That is the workflow's value
   demonstrated by its own absence.
2. **The D-TEAM-020 wording should be read as "observed live, not receipted."** The 62/62 green
   itself *is* receipted (`fm8.log` in the session's temp, and the suite re-run under G-08); what
   is not receipted is the proof that the suite can go red.
