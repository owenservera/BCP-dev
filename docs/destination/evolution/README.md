## Central question

**How may VIVIM change while remaining the same coherent, user-owned, evidence-backed system?**

## Three change classes

1. Deterministic maintenance — bounded repair/recomputation with no semantic invention.
2. Governed evolution — meaningful change under impact, compatibility, authority, verification, promotion, and rollback.
3. Constitutional evolution — change to the rules governing the first two classes; separately authorized.

## Research frontier

The initial hard problems are dynamic data/ontology evolution, identity reconciliation, relationship evolution, compatibility, change impact, temporal continuity, migration/recovery, evidence across versions, resource economics, self-extension, and constitutional anti-bypass.
