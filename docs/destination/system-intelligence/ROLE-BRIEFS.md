# System Intelligence — Investigator Roles

> **Classification: DERIVED — PROPOSED**

Six initial investigators are intentionally complementary.

## SI-01 — Ω Runtime

Source: current BCP-dev/omega-baseline/omega-final.

Deeply reconstruct boot/lifecycle/recovery, plugin/runtime boundary, composition, capability contracts, vault/persistence, evidence, law/authority hooks, execution/run, events/state, platform seam, and surfaces that expose canonical contracts.

Question:

> What computational primitives actually exist in current Ω, what are their invariants, and what do they depend on?

## SI-02 — Legacy VIVIM

Source: vivim-original-baseline/vivim-final-enhanced.

Broad then deep on product shell, Canvas/LivingCanvas, UnifiedEntry, workspaces/projects, conversations, providers/accounts/sessions, configuration, algorithms, fixtures/tests, and discovery/healing.

Question:

> What behavior or machinery did Legacy VIVIM learn that the destination should not accidentally lose?

Where valuable, identify preserve, harvest, rebuild, recompose, move, adapt, or reject.

## SI-03 — Ω Lineage

Source: standalone owenservera/vivim-omega versus BCP Ω.

Investigate shared files/trees, divergence points, historical decisions, additions/removals, supersessions, and rationale.

Question:

> How did Ω evolve into current BCP, and what historical intent explains today's shape?

Standalone Ω is historical/reference evidence, not current BCP authority.

## SI-04 — Product & Surface

Sources: destination docs, Legacy UX, current Ω surfaces.

Investigate Product Instance, world/workspace/canvas, Universal Entry, conversation/chat surface, identity/account/session presentation, lifecycle, continuity, user-visible errors/consent/evidence, direct manipulation, and canonical storage versus projection.

Question:

> What must the user actually experience, and which existing mechanisms can supply that behavior?

## SI-05 — Boundaries & Dependencies

Sources: all three lineages.

Focus on Provider/Account, Account/Session, Session/Browser, Capability/Realization, Intent/Capability, Capability/Authority, Work/Evidence, World/Surface, Plugin/Runtime, Product Instance/Persistence, and Provider/Routing.

Question:

> Where do hidden assumptions live between components that individually appear complete?

This role hunts cross-system contracts and missing dependencies.

## SI-06 — Red Team

Sources: all outputs plus destination/program docs.

Investigate contradictions, unsupported assumptions, lineage confusion, false canonicality, implementation/proof mismatch, missing product dependencies, missing external-reality dependencies, stale claims, and apparent-completeness traps.

Question:

> What would make the reconstructed system model wrong?

Challenge findings with evidence; do not silently overwrite them.
