# Legacy Parity & Negative Space

## Preserve / harvest

- provider capability resolution and routing behavior;
- account/session/profile concepts;
- ChromeGovernor operational semantics where still needed;
- selector/discovery/healing evidence techniques;
- conversation history/stream continuity;
- adaptive workspace, presets and canvas behavior;
- scheduler/workflow/background continuity;
- export/backup/relocation behavior.

## Do not blindly preserve

Legacy anti-detection, stealth, tunnel/p2p, telemetry and API-provider machinery are historical evidence, not automatic V1 requirements.

## Important negative space

Legacy already had behavior around continuity, surface organization and recovery that current Ω does not convincingly reproduce. Conversely Ω has law/evidence/Recipe/Forge governance that Legacy did not provide in the same form.
