# VIVIM Foundational Principles

> Classification: DERIVED — WORKING / DRAFT
> These are candidate gold standards for interactive shaping, not yet final law.

## 1. Sovereignty first
The person is the ultimate owner of their computing environment, durable data, rules, and interaction model.

## 2. My machine
The person's machine is the durable home of the sovereign environment. Local-first is a consequence of ownership, not merely a performance choice.

## 3. My internet
External providers, websites, and services remain external. VIVIM gives the person a sovereign relationship and control layer around them.

## 4. My accounts
Accounts remain owned by their providers, but their authenticated relationships, context, permissions, and authorized interactions can be represented and governed locally.

## 5. My data
Durable personal data stays under the person's control, is inspectable, portable, and recoverable.

## 6. My intelligence
Models are replaceable sources of reasoning or generation. They are not the sovereign authority.

## 7. My rules
The person controls meaningful capability boundaries, permissions, automation, retention, attention, and approval behavior.

## 8. No privileged user class
There is no separate administrator or developer class required to make the environment powerful. The person can access the system's fundamental powers through safe and intelligible mechanisms.

## 9. Everything is composable
Capabilities are provided by plugins and can be combined into new behavior. Automation is a composition of capabilities, triggers, policies, and work.

## 10. Plugin boundaries are explicit
A plugin declares what it can do, what it can access, what it needs, what effects it may cause, and how it is configured. Those boundaries are visible to the user.

## 11. Hyper-configurability is native
Meaningful aspects of the environment can be configured at fine granularity, including presentation, behavior, attention, automation, permissions, retention, provider choices, and composition.

## 12. Natural language is a native control surface
The prompt is not merely chat. It is a primary way to address the environment, configure it, create things, and delegate work.

## 13. The interaction layer belongs to the person
Surfaces, layouts, workspaces, representations, and interaction patterns can be shaped and composed rather than treated as immutable application boundaries.

## 14. Complexity stays underneath
The implementation may be extremely sophisticated. Ordinary users should not need to understand provider protocols, orchestration, parsers, realization internals, or storage machinery.

## 15. No mysterious agency
Important actions should have a discoverable actor, capability, authority, scope, and outcome. Background work is inspectable.

## 16. Evidence is not authority
Evidence may support a conclusion or action without granting permission to act. Representation, description, evidence, and authority remain distinct.

## 17. Continuity matters
The environment may continue authorized work while the person is away and should report meaningful changes, results, and pending decisions on return.

## 18. Evolution is native
The person can create, compose, configure, replace, repair, and extend the environment from within the environment.

## 19. Exit remains possible
Essential state must remain portable and recoverable. VIVIM must not become a new lock-in point.

## Gold-standard test
A future architecture or feature should be judged by whether it strengthens these principles without quietly introducing a hidden owner, privileged path, opaque authority, or irreversible dependency.
