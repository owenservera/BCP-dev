# VIVIM North Star

> Classification: DERIVED — WORKING / DRAFT
> Status: destination statement under interactive shaping.

## What are we building?

VIVIM is a sovereign personal computing environment.

Its foundational promise is:

**My machine. My internet. My accounts. My apps. My data. My intelligence. My rules. My interaction.**

The purpose is not simply to aggregate applications, store data locally, or add an AI assistant. The purpose is to give a person a single sovereign environment through which they can understand, organize, interact with, automate, and extend their digital world without having to carry the underlying complexity in their head.

## What sovereignty means here

The person's machine is the durable home of the environment.

External services remain external. VIVIM does not claim to own Google, GitHub, ChatGPT, Claude, email providers, or the internet. Instead, VIVIM owns the person's local relationship to those systems: their representation, context, capabilities, permissions, configuration, history, evidence, and authorized interactions.

The person's durable data should remain under their control, portable, inspectable, and recoverable.

The person's AI providers are replaceable sources of intelligence rather than sovereign authorities.

The person's rules govern what the environment and its delegated agents may do.

The interaction layer itself belongs to the person and can be shaped rather than treated as an immutable application surface.

## The environment

VIVIM should feel like a persistent digital environment rather than a collection of disconnected applications.

The person can encounter, organize, inspect, connect, converse with, act upon, delegate work to, and build upon things in that environment.

The environment remains active while the person is away. Authorized background work may continue, and the next arrival should include continuity: what changed, what happened, what was done, what needs attention, and what comes next.

## The central design bet

Extreme internal complexity should coexist with a simple human mental model.

The system may contain plugins, capability registries, provider realizations, deterministic tools, LLM reasoning, orchestration, evidence, policy, background execution, repair, and Forge machinery.

The person should primarily experience:

**my world → my context → what I want → what can be done → what I delegated → what happened → what I can do next.**

## V1, BCP, and Ω

VIVIM V1 is the first real prototype and therefore valuable evidence of what can work.

BCP is the transition and proof machinery used to reconcile, harvest, verify, and move toward the destination.

Ω supplies the strongest current destination-oriented architectural and governance material.

The destination is not required to preserve the historical shapes of any of those implementations. It is required to preserve the principles and useful capabilities that survive examination.
