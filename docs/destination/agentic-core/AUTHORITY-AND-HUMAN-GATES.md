# Authority and Human Gates

Authority belongs at the Capability/realization boundary under Ω Law, consent and principal context.

```
Plan → Capability resolution → Law/grant/consent/risk → ALLOW|REFUSE|HUMAN_REVIEW → realization
```

Scheduler, AgentDefinition, Plan and Surface cannot grant authority.

## Human gate

`WAITING_HUMAN` persists the exact proposed action, Work/Plan/Step/Attempt, capability/arguments (redacted), reason, expiration, decision authority and evidence refs. Approval resumes the same Work.

Human input may request parameters, choices, corrections, authentication or conflict resolution.

Approval is scoped and rechecked immediately before consequential dispatch. Revoked/expired authority fails closed.
