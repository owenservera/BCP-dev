# Human Router — Boundary Round 1

> For Owen as communication router between CFA agents and the Architecture Steward.

## Your job

You do not need to synthesize the agents' boundary work yourself.

For Round 1, your job is only to:
1. point each CFA at the common prompt and routing matrix;
2. let each CFA inspect its own domain plus assigned seams;
3. preserve each CFA's wording and evidence;
4. route explicit peer questions when useful;
5. bring completed declarations back to the Architecture Steward conversation.

Do **not** pre-resolve disagreements.

## One prompt for every CFA

Send this exact instruction to the agent, with the CFA-specific folder substituted:

> Read and execute:
> `AGENTS_CONTEXT/ARCHITECTURE_STEWARD/BOUNDARY-DESIGN-SYSTEM/ROUND-1-BOUNDARY-BOOTSTRAP.md`
>
> Your CFA assignment is defined in:
> `AGENTS_CONTEXT/ARCHITECTURE_STEWARD/BOUNDARY-DESIGN-SYSTEM/ROUND-1-CFA-MATRIX.md`
>
> Your Round 1 priority seams are listed in the routing matrix; do not perform work for other CFAs.
>
> Before acting, read your current CFA bootstrap/identity/current-state artifacts and the shared Boundary Protocol.
>
> Produce the required durable file:
> `AGENTS_CONTEXT/ARCHITECTURE_STEWARD/SUBAGENTS/<YOUR-CFA-FOLDER>/BOUNDARY-ROUND-1-DECLARATION-2026-09-26.md`
>
> Do not redesign your CFA mission, change Ω law, implement code, or resolve peer disputes unilaterally.
>
> When complete, report the required completion message specified by the common prompt.

## When an agent asks a peer a question

Route the question verbatim where practical.

Do not paraphrase a disputed semantic claim into an apparent agreement.

Use this pattern:

> CFA-XX has asked you to answer this boundary question:
>
> **[verbatim question]**
>
> Please answer from your own CFA responsibility and evidence. Do not assume CFA-XX's claim is correct.

Ask the responding CFA to preserve its answer in its own declaration or a clearly linked addendum.

## What you send back to the Architecture Steward

After several or all declarations exist, bring the repository state to the Steward and state:

> Round 1 declarations are now available in the CFA folders. Please inspect all available declarations against the Boundary Protocol, compare the two-sided seams, and produce the first cross-CFA reconciliation package. Do not activate a boundary where the two sides materially conflict.

You do not need to manually summarize every CFA.

## What happens next

The Steward will produce:
- a comparison of CFA claims;
- agreement/overlap/gap/conflict findings;
- BoundaryChallenges for material disagreements;
- a small Round 2 peer-reconciliation queue;
- the first candidate ACTIVE boundary records only where evidence and peer claims align.

## Do not do these things yet

- do not ask all agents to use identical internal methodologies;
- do not build the common `architecture.boundary` tool;
- do not merge CFA ownership claims;
- do not ask agents to rank one another;
- do not create a new permanent epistemic-integrity agent;
- do not modify Ω law;
- do not turn Commons messages into architectural truth.

## Router success condition

Round 1 is complete when the Steward has enough independent CFA declarations to compare both sides of the major seams without relying on the human router's interpretation.
