## Executive finding

The four declarations are substantially compatible at the level of first principles. No material direct contradiction requires immediate human-owner intervention.

The dominant issue is multi-dimensional overlap: identity, context, scope, evidence, effect, result/status, provenance and continuity appear in several CFA descriptions, but with different ownership dimensions.

Round 1 therefore produces candidate seams and bounded Round-2 questions. It does not activate shared boundaries.

## Cross-cutting findings

### Identity

No universal identity owner is justified. Current dimensions appear to be:

- World semantic identity/correspondence → CFA-01
- durable canonical record/revision/lineage → CFA-02
- semantic continuity across transformations + Intent/Plan meaning → CFA-03
- principal/authority identity at authorization boundary → CFA-04, consuming canonical identity from Data
- Work/Attempt identity → expected CFA-05
- capability/realization identity → expected CFA-06

Do not create a universal IdentityOwner or universal identity database from this round.

### Meaning

CFA-01 claims World-domain meaning. CFA-03 claims continuity of meaning across grounding, interpretation, Intent/Plan, execution meaning, evidence and representation. These are currently compatible rather than contradictory.

### Evidence and authority

All four declarations preserve the distinction:

Evidence can support a claim; evidence does not itself grant permission.

Representation, persistence or visibility does not itself create authority.

### Projection

World projections and Data projections are derived views whose basis and reconstructability remain explicit. Neither should silently become a second source of truth.

### Unknown / stale / conflicted

The common epistemic protocol is functioning as intended. Unknown, stale and conflicted states are being preserved rather than forced into binary success/failure or true/false.
