## Rationale (why this ranking, from the CFA-08 lens)

1. **Projection verifiability first.** CFA-08's central risk is semantic duplication through unverifiable surfaces. Ranks 1–4 make every projection claim mechanically checkable (drive it, see it, structure-dump it, rebuild it) before any product UI exists.
2. **Interaction convergence before richness.** Ranks 3 + 5 prove `gesture → typed handoff → owning path` with cheap deterministic artifacts (trees, logs) before live desktop/browser actuation (ranks 1, 6) is trusted with consequential gestures.
3. **Uncharacterized frontiers get probes, not builds.** Ranks 6–7 address the two least-characterized CFA-08 areas (native shell, notification delivery) with observation-first instruments, honoring the roadmap's "contract/falsifier before implementation" principle.
4. **Reuse over invention.** Ranks 9–10 operationalize already-ratified machinery (Commons CLI, `surfaceOpMeta`, D-436 gate) rather than proposing new frameworks — consistent with the roadmap's ADOPTED classifications.
5. **Deferred items stayed deferred.** Full frontend stack, cross-device sync tooling, and visual-polish frameworks are deliberately absent: the roadmap marks them NOT YET NEEDED, and nothing in the local-access transition changes the semantic-readiness precondition.
