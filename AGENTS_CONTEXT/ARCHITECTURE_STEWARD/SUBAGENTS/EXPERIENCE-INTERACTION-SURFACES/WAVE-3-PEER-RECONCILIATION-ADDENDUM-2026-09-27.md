## 3. Q08-01 — CFA-01 World / Space → truthful presentation

What minimum World/Space reference/result is required for truthful presentation, including resolved, ambiguous, stale, unresolvable and conflicted cases?

### Peer evidence

```
WorldReferenceResult
  subjectRef(s)
  worldMeaning
  resolutionState
  correspondenceState
  evidenceRefs
  sourceRefs
  freshness
  unknowns
  conflicts
  basisRef
```

CFA-01 also explicitly separates:

- World existence from observation;
- addressability from permission;
- visibility from nonexistence;
- scoped accessibility from authorization;
- World semantic meaning from reference-resolution state.

Its `resolutionState` vocabulary includes `RESOLVED | AMBIGUOUS | STALE | UNRESOLVABLE | CONFLICTED`.

CFA-03 has separately accepted this World-side result as the minimum interoperable seam contract, while keeping it a seam contract rather than a mandate for CFA-01 internals.

### CFA-08 response

Accepted for the presentation boundary.

The minimum World-side presentation basis is therefore:

`subjectRef(s) + worldMeaning + resolutionState + correspondenceState + evidence/source refs + freshness + unknown/conflict detail + basisRef`

CFA-08 may project these dimensions into a Surface/View, but must preserve their distinctions.

Presentation rules:

- `RESOLVED` may be rendered as a current subject reference only under its stated basis/freshness.
- `AMBIGUOUS` must remain visibly ambiguous or candidate-valued.
- `STALE` must not silently render as current truth.
- `UNRESOLVABLE` must not be rewritten as nonexistence.
- `CONFLICTED` must remain conflict-visible.
- `visible` and `accessible` are presentation/projection facts only within an explicitly stated scope/basis.
- `authorized` remains outside World presentation authority and comes from CFA-04.

### Classification

**RECONCILED** at the semantic/presentation handoff level.

Exact Surface payload fields, rendering choices and Space/Workspace projection schema remain implementation/design details outside this seam closure.

### Handoff

`CFA-01 WorldReferenceResult → CFA-08 projection/view`

Any semantic World mutation returns:

`Surface action → typed World semantic request → CFA-01-owned path`
