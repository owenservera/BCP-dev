## Rationale (why this ranking, CFA-05 lens)

The ordering follows the operating loop `FORM WORK → GATE → SCHEDULE/START → ATTEMPT → CHECKPOINT → VERIFY/RECONCILE → OUTCOME → EVIDENCE` by failure leverage under FULL LOCAL MACHINE ACCESS: first the durable subject itself (1), then bounded execution (2) and faithful reporting (3), then the completion gate that keeps autonomy honest (4). Recovery machinery (5, 6) outranks throughput machinery (7, 8) because an autonomous team that cannot survive its own crashes must not go fast. Duplicate-effect guards (9) precede terminal trust (10) because truthful Outcomes presuppose safe retries. Throughput without 1–6 is just faster loss of continuity — the exact failure the Work-centered identity exists to prevent ("the worker may disappear; the Work must remain").

Deliberately NOT in the top 10 (exclusions with reason): a general workflow/DAG engine (roadmap Deferred — evidence must prove necessity first); a second work/task database (non-scope — CFA-02 owns durability mechanics); a universal scheduler/event bus as a new layer (non-scope — Work-local continuity only); provider/account/session machinery (CFA-06); live authorization infrastructure (CFA-04); K0 enforcement primitives (CFA-10); surface/UI tooling (CFA-08); A2A-live transport and MCP mesh as such (needed, but transport/tool-provisioning, not work-lifecycle — they appear here only through their work-facing seams: envelopes, budgets, idempotency keys).
