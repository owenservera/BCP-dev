## Rationale (why this order)

1. **Realization first.** A capability with no valid realization candidate is
   unroutable; ranks 1–4 establish at least one live/browser, one provider-API,
   one resource-lifecycle, and one local-deterministic realization class.
2. **Evidence before autonomy.** Ranks 5–7 make every realization checkable
   (tests, discovery input, canonical record reads) before the team acts
   autonomously on the world.
3. **Account/routing before scale.** Ranks 8–9 close the two UNDERPROVEN rows
   closest to live user harm (R-077 account identity, R-082 routing/selection):
   secrets stay referenced, selection stays user-owned and auditable.
4. **Healing last, deliberately.** Rank 10 is instrumented observation, not an
   autonomous healing platform — the roadmap defers broad healing until the
   CFA-09 handoff is proven (roadmap "Deferred / Do Not Do").
