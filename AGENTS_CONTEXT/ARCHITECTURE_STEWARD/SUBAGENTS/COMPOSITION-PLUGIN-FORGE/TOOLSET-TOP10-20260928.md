## Rationale (why this ranking, from the CFA-07 lens)

1. **Machine access first (1–5), domain substrate second (6–9), the one justified build last (10).**
   Full-machine access changes the execution surface, not the architecture. Rows 1–5
   convert raw machine capability into governable team capability; rows 6–9 point
   that capability at the already-existing composition/Forge machinery instead of
   rebuilding it; row 10 is the single place the roadmap justifies new tooling.
2. **Do-not-rebuild discipline.** The roadmap's strategic implication is explicit:
   do not spend the next phase rebuilding Forge as if immature (Wave-0 proof is
   strong). Rows 6–8 are RUN-and-VERIFY-locally, not build. The team's Forge
   leverage comes from executing existing generation/proving paths on the local
   machine, where iteration is orders of magnitude faster than webapp round-trips.
3. **Replacement is the frontier.** M1 (identity), M2 (membrane), M3 (Forge),
   M5 (K1→K0 seam) all have strong existing substrates; M4 (replacement
   continuity) has none executable. Under the CFA-07 central question — assemble,
   extend, generate, prove, AND REPLACE while preserving semantics — the ranking
   weights the missing replacement proof above marginal improvements to generation.
4. **Symmetry is executable or it is nothing.** Rows 7+8 are paired deliberately:
   generation power without the refusal gate recreates exactly the privileged-SDK
   failure mode CFA-07 exists to challenge. Any local-team speedup of Forge output
   must be matched 1:1 by gate runs.
5. **The team models its domain.** Row 9 ranks above row 10 because a team that
   cannot govern its own candidate→admitted transitions has no standing to design
   composition governance. The Durable Completion Gate is CFA-07 semantics applied
   to ourselves.
