## Rationale (why this order)

Entry-first: meaning enters as language (1) and is resolved against knowledge (2);
only then is it worth persisting (3). Persistence is worthless without traceability
(4), and traceability is unverifiable without captured basis (5) and replay proof
(6). With the spine provable, the team-scale risks dominate: shared vocabulary (7),
fresh-agent intelligibility (8), representation forking (9), and continuous
falsification across concurrent writers (10). Each rank is ordered by blast radius
of its absence: a missing interpreter corrupts everything downstream; a missing
divergence monitor only slows detection of corruption the lower ranks already bound.
