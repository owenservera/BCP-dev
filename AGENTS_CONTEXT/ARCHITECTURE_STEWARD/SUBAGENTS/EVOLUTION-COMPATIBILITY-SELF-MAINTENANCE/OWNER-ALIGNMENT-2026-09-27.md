## 14. Self-maintenance and safe automation

**ALIGNED WITH A BOUNDED AUTOMATION MODEL.**

Automatic maintenance is permitted only when:

1. the semantics are already known;
2. the operation is inside a declared safe envelope;
3. the result is reversible or reconstructible to the required degree;
4. the impact is sufficiently bounded;
5. required authority/runtime constraints remain satisfied;
6. verification can be performed.

Examples include bounded rebuild/recompute/reindex/reconnect/retry/reconcile, known migration paths, known provider-repair consequences and disposable projection reconstruction.

Outside that envelope, the system must stop at:
- proposal;
- refusal;
- quarantine;
- or explicit human/owner gate.

The ability to automate does not create permission to automate.
