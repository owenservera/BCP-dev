# Ω End-State Build Team — Operating Principles Seed

> Status: STARTER QUESTIONS, NOT RATIFIED PRINCIPLES
> Date: 2026-09-28

This file exists to make explicit that the team itself must discover its operating principles.

Do not treat any item below as settled law.

The team should decide, with evidence, how strongly it wants to optimize for:

- end-to-end product value over subsystem completion;
- fresh design over historical compatibility;
- measurable proof over documentation confidence;
- fast learning over premature architectural commitment;
- local autonomy over centralized coordination;
- composability over fixed product structure;
- replaceability over sunk-cost preservation;
- explicit uncertainty over false certainty;
- whole-system coherence over local optimization.

The team may reject, refine, add to, or replace every item here.
