## Round objective

This is the **first real strategic roadmap round** for the ratified CFA constellation.

Each CFA independently produces its own high-level conceptual roadmap covering:

- strategic objective;
- 3–7 core milestones;
- milestone success criteria;
- dependencies;
- tooling/substrate required;
- strategic design decisions;
- first-pass peer intelligence needed at **each milestone**;
- explicit inherited-plan adoption/defer/reject decisions.

The output is not a backlog and not implementation authorization.

## Independence rule

Launch all ten sessions in parallel.

Do not synchronize their roadmaps before first-pass completion.

Each session may read existing peer artifacts, but must not use a peer's newly-produced round result to shape its own first-pass roadmap. We want ten independent models first.
