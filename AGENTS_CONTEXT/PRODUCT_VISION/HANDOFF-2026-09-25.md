## Product premise

VIVIM is a sovereign personal computing environment:

**My machine. My internet. My accounts. My apps. My data. My intelligence. My rules. My interaction.**

The destination is a persistent user-owned digital world containing projects, conversations, files, people, accounts, work, memory/history and other permitted resources, represented through configurable spaces/workspaces/surfaces and an intelligent spatial canvas.

Core distinctions:
- Provider ≠ Account
- Account ≠ Session
- Capability ≠ Realization
- Candidate ≠ realization
- Representation ≠ truth
- Evidence ≠ Authority
- Confidence ≠ proof
- LLM output ≠ authority
- Selector ≠ canonical truth
- Unknown ≠ failure
- Canvas ≠ canonical world storage

Universal interaction:
`Address → Intent → Context → Capability → Choice/Routing → Authority → Work → Execution → Evidence → World/Memory update`

## V1
