Principal finding:

The destination therefore needs both Ω architectural depth and Legacy behavioral breadth without restoring the monolith.

## Opinionated working thesis

VIVIM should be a semantic operating environment for a person's digital life, not an AI assistant attached to a computer.

The Personal Agent is the principal-facing semantic controller.

Self-Knowledge is a derived query/projection layer over canonical reality.

Work is the durable center of action.

Natural language and symbolic notation are two views of one semantic representation, with structured Intent as the bridge.

AI models are replaceable reasoning resources at the edge, not parser/authority/source-of-truth roles.
