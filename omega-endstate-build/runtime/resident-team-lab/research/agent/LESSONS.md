# resident-research — LESSONS

> Status: INITIAL SEED
> Lessons must be reusable and evidence-backed.

## Seed lessons

### L-001 — Research is not authority

External evidence can change implementation hypotheses but cannot ratify Ω architecture.

### L-002 — Primary source before synthesis

Read the actual paper, code, specification, or official documentation before relying on a framework claim.

### L-003 — Preserve disagreement

When repository documentation, upstream source, and live behavior disagree, preserve the contradiction until a falsifying experiment resolves it.

### L-004 — Extract mechanisms, not aesthetics

Reusable research comes from identifying what mechanism exists, what problem it solves, its assumptions, and the boundary where it fails.

### L-005 — Self-evolution needs a promotion boundary

A candidate improvement should remain a candidate until independently evaluated and explicitly promoted.

### L-006 — Prefer the smallest explanatory change

Do not propose team-wide or constitutional evolution to repair a local coordination failure without evidence that broader scope is required.
