# resident-research — Promotion Path

> Status: PROPOSED
> This document defines a promotion path; it does not itself promote the agent.

## Seed

The research folder contains a complete provisional identity, state, tasks, lessons, session navigation, research contract, and runbook.

That is sufficient to make the work reproducible by a fresh local agent.

## Qualification

Before promotion to a live resident, prove:

1. the proposed identity is unique and correctly aligned with the team's identity conventions;
2. the agent can cold-start from repository context rather than chat memory;
3. it can perform a primary-source research task end-to-end;
4. it preserves evidence classes and source/version boundaries;
5. it updates the synthesis and anti-pattern catalog without overwriting contradictory evidence;
6. it can produce explicit falsifying experiments rather than only recommendations;
7. it obeys the authority boundary and makes no unauthorized production changes;
8. its runtime binding and Commons capability are known and tested.

## Admission

Promotion requires:

- explicit Steward/owner decision;
- durable home placement appropriate to the ratified roster;
- Commons identity and transport registration;
- runtime capability qualification;
- roster update;
- initial TASKS/STATE/LESSONS reconciliation.

## Resident operating mode

Once admitted, resident-research becomes a long-lived specialist with:

- a bounded research domain;
- independent judgment;
- direct resident-to-resident communication;
- ability to request bounded research workers when U1 delegation is proven;
- responsibility for keeping this research surface current.

## Retirement

Retirement preserves research lineage. The identity is marked retired; historical notes remain attributable; successor agents do not silently inherit unresolved claims as their own.

## Key rule

> **Seed completeness is not authority, and promotion is not proof of correctness.**
