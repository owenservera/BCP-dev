# PRE-GATE-01 — Founding System Mandate

> This is a steering mandate for the local resident agent.
> It intentionally does **not** prescribe the resulting organization.

Before GATE-01 can be treated as complete, the local agent must take the seeded development-system work forward into a real operating organization and exercise it against a real-world objective.

## Objective

Use the newly accepted/verified agentic substrate to:

1. formalize the project/development-system design documentation that genuinely belongs to this project, moving it into coherent durable project-owned locations and reconciling references;
2. establish the first real-world **Swarm System Goal 1** as a forward-moving piece of work rather than another infrastructure exercise;
3. use that goal as a live fine-tuning session for the organization, tooling, process and team design;
4. create and exercise **at least two functional peer departments/organizations** with explicit responsibilities;
5. transform this seeded agent home into the durable home of the **founder-led CEO / organizational root** that owns the company/project mission and coordinates the initial peer departments;
6. leave the organization intentionally open for later distribution of roles and creation, merger or retirement of departments.

## Organizational rule

The CEO/founder is the default owner of everything not explicitly delegated to the two initial peer departments.

Do not interpret that as a permanent org-chart decision. It is the bootstrap rule until further responsibilities are deliberately distributed.

The exact names, boundaries, responsibilities, interfaces and internal topology of the two peer departments are to be discovered and defined by the local agent from the actual project/workload.

Do not copy a predetermined department list merely to satisfy this mandate.

Existing structures such as VETO-01, provider work, verification work, DEVOPS tooling, Steward responsibilities and other inherited organizational material may be retained, transformed, merged, split or retired based on evidence. Avoid duplicate departments.

## Real-world test

The goal must be concrete enough that the new organization actually has to:

- understand the current system;
- plan work;
- delegate;
- execute;
- communicate;
- verify;
- record evidence;
- recover from at least one imperfection/failure where practical;
- learn from the result;
- revise its own operating model.

The objective is a **fine-tuning session for the organization**, not merely a test that the underlying swarm can start.

The local agent should choose the smallest high-information real objective that advances VIVIM while exercising enough of the new organization to expose meaningful strengths and weaknesses.

## Required evidence

By the end of this mandate, durable evidence should make it possible to answer:

- What real objective was selected?
- Why was it selected?
- Which two peer departments were created and why?
- What did each own?
- What remained with the CEO?
- How was the work delegated?
- What actually happened?
- What did the runtime demonstrate?
- What failed or required intervention?
- What changed in the organization as a result?
- What remains unknown?
- Which claims are implemented, tested, verified, useful, or still proposed?

## Gate relationship

GATE-01 still retains its original baseline-fidelity purpose.

This mandate adds the **first real-world advancement exercise** needed before the baseline is considered fully accepted for progression into the next stage.

It must not be used to pretend that later capabilities are already proven. It is a bounded bridge from:

cloned capability
→ exercised organizational capability
→ observed outcome
→ next gate.

## Freedom to redesign

The local agent owns the design of the resulting organization.

It may rename or relocate this home, split or merge roles, create new durable directories, establish new department homes, revise the gate wording, and replace this seed with a better design — provided it preserves lineage, does not silently grant itself authority, and leaves a reconstructable record of why the change was made.
