# VETO-01 — Conceptual Commitment Boundary Discovery

> Status: LIVE RESEARCH TARGET
> Primary company mission: **full VIVIM beta ready to distribute for free**

## Why this boundary comes first

The earliest useful point for VETO-01 is not code integration.

It is the transition from:

> "we are exploring an idea"

to:

> "we are investing enough in this idea that it is becoming an implementation commitment."

This boundary is currently unknown.

The purpose of this experiment is to discover it.

## What we are not trying to determine yet

We are not trying to define:

- the ultimate VETO-01 architecture;
- the complete development lifecycle;
- universal governance rules;
- the final automatic trigger;
- the eventual number of authority levels.

We are trying to learn one practical thing:

> **What observable evidence tells us that conceptual work has become consequential enough to merit independent review?**

## Working hypothesis

A useful review boundary will correlate more strongly with **accumulated commitment** than with raw activity.

Activity matters, but activity alone is weak.

For example:

- a long research document can still be cheap exploration;
- a short document can encode a major architectural commitment;
- ten edits can represent healthy refinement;
- three cross-linked artifacts may create substantial downstream dependency.

Therefore the experiment should observe both:

### Activity signals

- edits;
- document count;
- revision frequency;
- discussion volume;
- elapsed effort.

### Commitment signals

- explicit roadmap linkage;
- dependency creation;
- allocation of agents or implementation capacity;
- irreversible decisions;
- durable cross-links;
- claims that other work depends upon;
- transition from alternatives to a selected approach;
- preparation of implementation tasks.

## Review candidate generation

Initially, candidate generation should be lightweight.

Possible source:

> "This work shows several commitment signals. Should VETO-01 review it?"

The owner or responsible lead decides.

No automatic review is required.

## Candidate record

A candidate should preserve:

- candidate ID;
- observed signals;
- relevant work reference;
- current maturity state;
- who noticed it;
- whether review was requested;
- VETO-01 result;
- owner decision;
- later outcome.

## What the experiment should learn

For each candidate, eventually ask:

- Was this actually a meaningful decision boundary?
- Was review useful?
- Did VETO-01 surface something material?
- Was the context sufficient?
- Was review too early?
- Was review too late?
- Did review prevent cost?
- Did review introduce avoidable delay?
- Which signals were predictive?
- Which signals were noise?

## Evolution path

The experimental sequence is:

### Stage 0 — Human recognition

Humans simply notice that work appears mature enough and request VETO-01.

### Stage 1 — Signal-assisted recognition

A lightweight observer suggests review candidates.

### Stage 2 — Measured stage trigger

The organization establishes evidence-backed conditions that automatically request review.

### Stage 3 — Learned / adaptive trigger

The trigger becomes more context-sensitive because the system has enough outcome history to improve it.

### Stage 4 — Autonomous governor activation

VETO-01 can initiate review itself when it observes a sufficiently strong condition.

These stages concern **trigger autonomy**, not veto authority.

## Constraint

The experiment must remain subordinate to the company mission.

If candidate detection, review traffic, or governance overhead begins consuming material development capacity without measurable benefit, the experiment itself becomes subject to review.

## Long-horizon value

Even if the company never builds a fully autonomous governor, learning where conceptual commitment boundaries occur can improve:

- planning;
- research discipline;
- organizational design;
- context allocation;
- decision traceability;
- implementation readiness;
- beta delivery.

That makes the experiment useful independent of whether VETO-01 ever reaches code-level blocking authority.
