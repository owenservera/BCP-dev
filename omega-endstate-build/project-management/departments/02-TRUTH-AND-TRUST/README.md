# Department 02 — Truth & Trust

Owns the mechanisms that protect the trust chain: evidence, verification, gates, governance safeguards, falsification and durable state of what is actually known.

This department does not become the product manager or truth authority by declaration. Its job is to make claims auditable and consequential work challengeable.
