## Synthesis

No source is a complete blueprint.

The most complementary combination is:

`Meta-Team` -> how distributed experience becomes useful evidence
`OMC` -> how identity/runtime/organization can be separated
`Swarm Skills` -> how coordination can become a portable versioned asset
`SwarmAgentic` -> how alternative organizations can be experimentally searched
`ClawTeam` -> how agents can practically operate spawn/isolation/dependency primitives

Ω can therefore use each source at a different architectural layer rather than creating one composite framework.
