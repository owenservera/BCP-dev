# COORD-01 / LOGIC Swim Lane

This is the durable home for the newly assigned **Internal Consistency** responsibility.

## Current responsibility

Track and flag internal inconsistencies across what the team produces and says, including:

- claims made during exploration and claiming;
- design and architecture statements;
- requirements and constraints;
- plans and intended work;
- decisions and commitments;
- later statements about what was done.

The first boundary is internal coherence.

This lane does **not** assume responsibility for determining external factual truth.

## Design status

**V1 is not yet designed.**

This directory is intentionally the place where COORD-01 and the owner will develop the responsibility together from observed team output.

Do not prematurely inherit the VETO-01 operating model, taxonomy, authority model or workflow merely because the two responsibilities are related.

## Core distinction

The responsibility is to detect and flag when the team's own evolving body of statements cannot coherently stand together.

Detection is not adjudication.
Inconsistency is not necessarily external falsehood.
A possible contradiction may instead be supersession, scope difference, ambiguity, definition drift, or an unresolved relationship.

Further design should emerge from real examples.
