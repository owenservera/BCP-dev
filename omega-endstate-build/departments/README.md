# Ω End-State Build — Departments

The organizational control plane is expressed as departments, not as one undifferentiated `agents/` tree.

## Department 01 — Research & Alignment

Purpose: research, architectural reasoning, live-environment investigation and internal alignment.

Current resident surfaces:

- `COORD-01/LOGIC/` — internal consistency and alignment lane.
- `PROV-01/` — provider / live-environment investigation.

## Department 02 — Truth & Trust

Purpose: independent challenge, verification, governance safeguards and trust-chain protection.

Current resident surfaces:

- `VETO/` — VETO / Mission Governor home surface.
- `VER-01/` — independent verification.

The authoritative VETO research corpus remains:

`research/departments/veto-governor/`

## Department 03 — CEO & MVP Builder

Purpose: founder-level product ownership, coordination and the machinery required to turn the company mission into a distributable VIVIM beta.

Current resident surfaces:

- `STEW-01/` — end-state build Steward.
- `DEVOPS-01/` — agentic development tooling and the seeded founder-led organizational-root path.

## Identity versus organization

Resident role IDs remain useful execution identities. A role belongs to exactly one department home, while execution still occurs from its own isolated worktree or clone.

The department structure is organizational context. It is not a substitute for evidence, authority records, workspace isolation or Git lineage.
