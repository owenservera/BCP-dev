# Vision

VIVIM is a sovereign, local-first personal computing environment for working across AI providers and the wider digital world.

The end state is a single personal surface where the user can:
- work across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other providers without surrendering ownership;
- preserve conversations, context, memory, work and evidence locally;
- route work across providers, accounts, sessions and capabilities;
- interact with real web applications, not only APIs;
- maintain durable continuity across sessions and runtimes;
- understand where information came from and what authority supports action;
- evolve the system through governed plugins, capabilities and experiments.

The product is not merely an AI wrapper. It is a personal world, execution environment and durable relationship layer.

The development system follows the same principle: local, inspectable, recoverable, evidence-grounded and capable of improving itself without becoming its own mission.

The immediate destination is concrete:

**Full VIVIM beta, ready to distribute for free.**

The organization exists to make that outcome increasingly achievable—not to become the product.
