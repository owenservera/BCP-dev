# VG-0001 — Audit the Entire Swarm Agent System

Status: IN_PROGRESS
Claimed by: VETO-01 / COORD-01 audit session
Requester: OWNER
Class: AUDIT, RESEARCH
Created: 2026-09-28

## Objective

Independently audit the complete swarm and agent-development substrate relevant to the Ω End-State Build and determine what should be retained, challenged, tested, deferred, or rejected in service of the free full-beta mission.

## Target

Audit the entire relevant agentic substrate, including:

- vendored opencode-swarm;
- resident-team lab;
- native OpenCode Task/subagent semantics;
- current OpenCode permission and plugin behavior;
- existing BCP Agent Commons;
- existing mainline Steward/CFA/worker organization;
- worker model and delegation envelopes;
- memory/context mechanisms;
- communication mechanisms;
- task/work representations;
- current experimental governance;
- organizational research and anti-patterns.

## Mission connection

The agentic system is an enabling mechanism.

The governing test is whether it materially accelerates preparation of the full VIVIM beta ready for free distribution without creating greater coordination and complexity cost than it removes.

## Audit stance

Do not begin by proposing a replacement architecture.

First reconstruct current reality.

Then identify:

1. capabilities already present;
2. capabilities proven versus merely documented;
3. contradictions between source, documentation and runtime;
4. recurring transaction costs;
5. missing organizational capabilities;
6. useful reusable mechanisms;
7. anti-pattern exposure;
8. high-value experiments;
9. possible veto-worthy commitments;
10. unanswered questions.

## Required evidence

Inspect the current repository.

For runtime claims, inspect exact installed/current source and perform live tests where possible.

For external claims, prefer primary sources.

Separate OBSERVED, VERIFIED, INFERRED, UNKNOWN and REFUTED.

## Required deliverable

Create:

results/VG-0001-entire-swarm-agent-system-audit.md

The result must include:

- executive disposition;
- current-system map;
- capability inventory;
- evidence/proof matrix;
- organizational topology assessment;
- context/memory assessment;
- delegation/authority assessment;
- tool/capability assessment;
- anti-pattern assessment;
- transaction-cost assessment;
- current gaps;
- candidate experiments;
- VETO_PROPOSED items, if any;
- NO_VETO areas where appropriate;
- recommended next research tasks;
- explicit distinction between present beta usefulness and long-horizon organizational potential.

## Independence

Do not treat implementation-team documentation as sufficient proof.

Do not rely on another agent's conclusion as evidence.

Do not treat convergence between agents as validation.

## Completion condition

The audit is complete when a fresh session could reconstruct the evidence and understand why each conclusion was reached without needing this conversation.
