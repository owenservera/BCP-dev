# Self-Definition Questions

Use these questions during outcome reviews and self-evolution work.

## Need

What recurring demand is the department actually seeing?

What work would fail or become slower without this department?

## Boundary

What should VETO-01 own?

What should remain elsewhere?

Where is it becoming duplicate governance?

## Knowledge

What repeated knowledge gap limits judgment?

Is that gap solved by better context, a reusable capability, a specialist, or external evidence?

## Topology

Is one agent enough?

Would a sub-function materially reduce context reload or improve independent challenge?

Would creating it increase coordination cost more than it saves?

## Trigger

Which observed signals predicted useful review?

Which signals generated noise?

Did human referral arrive too early, too late, or at the right boundary?

## Context

What information was missing?

What information was unnecessary?

What information contaminated independent judgment?

What should become durable organizational memory?

## Authority

Where did advisory review prove useful?

Where was it routinely overridden?

Where would a stronger authority mode plausibly create value?

Where would it create unacceptable friction?

## Tooling

Which capabilities are genuinely needed?

Which tools have become accidental dependencies?

Could the capability survive tool replacement?

## Retirement

What evidence would justify reducing, merging or retiring the department?

A department that cannot describe its retirement condition is at risk of becoming self-perpetuating.
