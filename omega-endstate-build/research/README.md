# Ω End-State Build — Research Lab

> Status: EXPERIMENTAL CONTROL SURFACE
> Primary company mission for this phase: **full VIVIM beta ready to distribute for free**

## Purpose

This research lab exists to discover practices, mechanisms, organizational forms and development-system capabilities that can materially accelerate the primary mission.

The lab is not required to discover or implement the ultimate VIVIM architecture, and it is not authorized to turn an interesting research direction into product architecture merely because it is intellectually attractive.

The governing hierarchy is:

1. Primary mission: get the full VIVIM beta ready to distribute for free.
2. Enabling objective: discover and build only the development capabilities that materially improve progress toward that mission.
3. Long-horizon research: investigate what a self-defining, self-evolving, self-governing development organization could become, without assuming that the company will build the ultimate version during this roadmap phase.

## Research operating rule

Every research item should distinguish:

- evidence from hypothesis;
- present beta relevance from long-horizon potential;
- smallest useful experiment from full-system implementation;
- measured acceleration from assumed acceleration.

A research result should normally end in one of:

- ADOPT-NOW — evidence supports a bounded implementation that helps current beta work;
- EXPERIMENT-NOW — a small test can cheaply establish whether it helps;
- WATCH — strategically interesting but not justified for current implementation;
- REJECT — evidence or economics do not justify further investment.

## Research as a living subsystem

The lab itself is an experimental organizational seed.

It may discover and test:

- new research roles;
- recursive specialist/department formation;
- dynamic context and memory structures;
- delegation and authority mechanisms;
- collaboration patterns;
- independent challenge and verification;
- organizational learning and self-evolution;
- governance and governance-of-governance;
- fungible capability interfaces;
- mechanisms for measuring coordination transaction cost.

Its own organization may evolve when recurring workload justifies it.

## Current experiment

The departments/veto-governor area contains the first explicit virtual-department seed: an opinionated mission governor with one organizational power — veto.

Its initial authority is deliberately advisory:

> it may propose a veto to the owner, but it may not enforce one.

That gives us a live way to study whether veto authority accelerates the beta mission before granting any runtime blocking power.
